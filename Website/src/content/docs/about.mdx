**Partiels** is a digital audio file analysis application for signal processing researchers, musicologists, composers, sound designers, etc., offering a dynamic interface for exploring the content and characteristics of sounds.

## Credits

Design, architecture and development by Pierre Guillot at the IMR department of [IRCAM](https://www.ircam.fr).
Contributions by Thomas Barbé, Nolan Dupont, and Guilherme Zanchetta.

### Models

The language models used by the AI agent are not distributed with Partiels: they are downloaded on demand from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/).

- **[Qwen3.5](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B)** and **[Qwen3.8](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B)** by Qwen Team, Alibaba Cloud, under the Apache 2.0 license, with the GGUF versions by [Unsloth](https://huggingface.co/unsloth).
- **[BGE-M3](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-m3)** under the MIT license and **[BGE Reranker v2 M3](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3)** under the Apache 2.0 license by BAAI, with the GGUF versions by [GPUStack](https://huggingface.co/gpustack).
