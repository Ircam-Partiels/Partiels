**Partiels** is a digital audio file analysis application for signal processing researchers, musicologists, composers, sound designers, etc., offering a dynamic interface for exploring the content and characteristics of sounds.

## Credits

Design, architecture and development by Pierre Guillot at the IMR department of [IRCAM](https://www.ircam.fr).
