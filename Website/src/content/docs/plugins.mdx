Partiels uses the [Vamp plugin technology](https://www.vamp-plugins.org/), an audio processing plugin system developed at the Centre for Digital Music, Queen Mary, University of London, to extract descriptive information from audio data.

Alongside Partiels, a wide range of analyses based on audio engines developed at IRCAM and elsewhere are ported to Vamp plugins.

## Installation

The installers copy the plugins to the default Vamp folder of your system, where Partiels finds them at startup:

- **macOS**: `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Vamp`. Plugins that require a more recent version of macOS or an Apple Silicon processor are only offered when they are compatible with your Mac.
- **Windows**: `C:\Program Files\Vamp Plugins`. The plugins can be uninstalled individually from the Windows settings.
- **Linux**: `$HOME/vamp`. Run the `Install.sh` script of the archive (with the `--all` option to install all the plugins), and the `Uninstall.sh` script to remove them.

You can change the folders in which Partiels looks for plugins in the [plugins settings](../manual/#13-plugins-settings).

:::caution
Some Vamp plugins may not support all the configurations and may have other restrictions.
:::

## Other plugins

Many other analysis plugins are listed on the [Vamp plugins website](https://www.vamp-plugins.org/). The plugins available on internet are also listed in the new track panel of Partiels, with a link to download them.
